This policy explains what personal data the Hevalo bot on Discord ("Hevalo" or "the bot") processes, why it does so, and what rights you have. It covers the bot only. The Hevalo app at [hevalo.app](https://hevalo.app) and this website have their own privacy policies.

## Summary

- Hevalo does not read the messages in your server. It sees the commands you use, the buttons you press, the messages you choose to translate, and the basic information Discord shares about members.
- It stores your Discord user ID and username, the language you choose, and your learning progress: XP, level, streaks, quiz results and achievements.
- For each server, it stores the settings its administrators choose and the moderation records its moderators create, such as warnings.
- What you type into the AI commands, and messages you choose to translate, are sent to Google's Gemini service to produce the answer. Hevalo does not store them.
- We do not sell personal data, show advertising or build profiles.
- You can ask to see or delete your data at any time by emailing [privacy@zagrosian.com](mailto:privacy@zagrosian.com).

## Who we are

Zagrosian, based in the Netherlands, operates Hevalo and is responsible for the personal data described in this policy (the "controller" under the General Data Protection Regulation, or GDPR).

For anything in this policy, email [privacy@zagrosian.com](mailto:privacy@zagrosian.com). Please include your Discord user ID, so we can find your data.

Hevalo runs on Discord, which is provided by Discord Inc. Discord processes your data under its own [privacy policy](https://discord.com/privacy). This policy covers only what Hevalo itself does.

## What Hevalo processes and why

### Your Discord identity

When you use a command or a button, or join a server where Hevalo is a member, Discord shares your user ID, username, display name and avatar, your roles in that server, and when your account was created. Hevalo stores your user ID and username, so it can keep your progress and settings and link records to you.

**Legal basis:** providing the service you ask for (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

### Learning progress and preferences

Hevalo stores your XP and level, your current and longest daily streak, when you last completed the daily challenge, how many quiz questions you answered and how many correctly, the answers you give, the achievements you unlock, and the language you choose with `/ziman`. Your progress is the same in every server where you use Hevalo. Other members can see your XP, level and streak through `/profile` and `/leaderboard`.

**Legal basis:** providing the service you ask for (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

### Things you ask Hevalo to keep

- **Reminders** (`/remind`): the text, the channel and the time. A reminder is deleted as soon as it has been delivered.
- **Suggestions** (`/suggest`): the text, which is also posted in the server's suggestions channel if it has one.

**Legal basis:** providing the service you ask for (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

### AI features

When you use `/ask`, `/translate`, `/explain` or `/tutor`, or the *Wergerîne bo Kurmancî* and *Translate to English* menus on a message, Hevalo sends the text (your question, or the message you selected) to Google's Gemini API, together with your language setting, to produce the answer. Hevalo does not store the text or the answer: the answer is posted in Discord, like any message.

Google processes this content under its own terms. For the version of the service we currently use, Google may keep the content and use it to improve its products, which can include review by people. **Please do not include personal or sensitive information in what you send to the AI features.** A message you translate may have been written by someone else: keep in mind that it is sent to Google too.

**Legal basis:** providing the service you ask for (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) and, for messages written by others, the legitimate interest of members and of Hevalo in understanding what is said in a server (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

### Music

When you use `/play`, Hevalo searches YouTube for what you asked for and streams the audio into the voice channel. YouTube receives the search and the address of our server, not yours. While a song plays, your username is shown as the person who requested it. Nothing about the music is stored once the queue ends.

### Servers, moderation and safety

For each server, Hevalo stores the server's ID and name and the settings its administrators choose: the language, the channels for welcomes, suggestions, the mod log and the word of the day, the welcome message, the role for new members and whether raid protection is on.

When moderators use Hevalo's moderation commands, Hevalo stores a record of the action: which member it concerned, which moderator took it, the reason and the time. Warnings are kept so that moderators can see a member's history and so that repeated warnings can lead to an automatic timeout. Actions are also posted in the server's mod-log channel, if it has one.

To protect servers against raids, Hevalo counts how many members join within a short time (in memory only, never stored) and posts a notice in the mod log when an account younger than three days joins. The AutoMod rules that Hevalo can set up are run by Discord itself: Discord, not Hevalo, checks messages against them.

The administrators and moderators of each server decide how these tools are used there.

**Legal basis:** our legitimate interest, and that of each server, in keeping communities safe and well run (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

### Technical logs

To keep Hevalo running and to fix problems, its logs record events such as which command was used, user and server IDs, and errors. We keep logs only as long as we need them to investigate problems, and use them for nothing else.

**Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in operating a secure and reliable service (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing, apart from the automatic timeout after repeated warnings, which your server's moderators can lift at any time.

## Who we share data with

We do not sell or rent personal data. We share it only as far as needed to provide Hevalo:

- **Discord Inc.** carries every message and interaction between you and Hevalo.
- **Google** provides the Gemini API for the AI features, as described above. See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the [Gemini API Additional Terms of Service](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms).
- **YouTube** (Google) receives music searches, without your identity.

Hevalo and its database run on servers that Zagrosian controls in the European Union, currently in the Netherlands.

We may also disclose personal data where the law requires it, or where it is needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

## International transfers

Discord and Google are based in the United States and may process data there. They rely on safeguards recognised under the GDPR, such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## How long we keep data

- **Your profile, progress and language:** until you ask us to delete them.
- **Reminders:** until they are delivered.
- **Suggestions:** until you or the server ask us to delete them.
- **Server settings:** while the server uses Hevalo. If Hevalo is removed from a server, its settings stay until the server's owner asks us to delete them.
- **Moderation records:** as long as the server uses them. Moderators can clear a member's warnings with `/warnings clear`, and the server's owner can ask us to delete all of the server's records.
- **AI requests and music searches:** not stored by Hevalo.
- **Games in progress, cooldowns and music queues:** kept in memory only, and forgotten when the bot restarts.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- access the personal data we hold about you;
- have inaccurate data corrected;
- have your data erased;
- restrict how we process your data;
- object to processing based on our legitimate interests;
- receive your data in a portable format, where this applies.

To exercise any of these rights, email [privacy@zagrosian.com](mailto:privacy@zagrosian.com) with your Discord user ID. We will respond within one month, and may ask you to confirm that the Discord account is yours. If you ask us to delete moderation records about you, we will weigh your request against the server's interest in keeping its community safe, and tell you what we decided and why.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In the Netherlands this is the [Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens](https://www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl/). You can also contact the authority in the EU country where you live or work. We would appreciate the chance to address your concern first.

## Removing Hevalo

A server's administrators can remove Hevalo at any time in the server's settings. If you added Hevalo to your own account, you can remove it under *Authorized Apps* in your Discord settings. Removing Hevalo stops it from receiving new data; to have stored data deleted, email us as described above.

## Children

Discord requires its users to be at least 13 years old, or older in some countries. Hevalo is meant for people who meet Discord's minimum age, and we do not knowingly collect data from children below it.

## Security

Hevalo connects to Discord and Google only over encrypted connections. Its database cannot be reached from the internet, and only Zagrosian has access to it. If you find a security issue, please read our [security policy](/security).

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Hevalo, the services it uses or the law change. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated. If we make significant changes, we will say so clearly on this page.
