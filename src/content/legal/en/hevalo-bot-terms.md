You are responsible for how Hevalo is used in your server, including the moderation actions your moderators take with it and the content of your welcome messages and announcements. Where needed, let your members know that the server uses Hevalo, for example by linking to its [privacy policy](/hevalo-bot-privacy).

Hevalo's safety tools (AutoMod rules, raid protection and escalating warnings) reduce risk, but they cannot catch everything and they do not replace your moderators.

They are not professional advice: check anything that matters. Do not send personal or sensitive information to the AI features. Google's [Generative AI Prohibited Use Policy](https://policies.google.com/terms/generative-ai/use-policy) also applies to what you ask.
